**[Care Touch Cleaning](https://caretouchcleaning.com)** and **[Copa Sport Store](https://copasportstore.com.br)** are both live and both mine to run. Care Touch is a US cleaning company whose entire digital operation I built and still maintain — a Next.js site on Cloudflare Pages with city and service landing pages and structured data, an AI concierge on Cloudflare Workers AI that fields enquiries and routes intent, and a content studio producing the brand's social output, across three repositories. Copa is a Brazilian football-kit retailer I designed, built and operate end to end: a Next.js storefront on Cloudflare Workers over an Express and Prisma API on Fly.io, with Pix, card and boleto through Mercado Pago, automated catalogue ingestion under a margin-governed pricing engine, an order lifecycle that mails the customer at every transition, payment reconciliation with recovery for orders stranded between providers, and a reseller programme carrying commission. Deploys, backups, reconciliation, error tracking and uptime are still mine.

Before that I spent a year on **Toygers**, a Telegram Mini App on TON for phygital collectibles — art-toy NFTs paired one-to-one with NFC-chipped physical pieces, sold, traded and redeemed in-app. I owned the Mini App end to end as its principal author, 502 of 892 commits: storefront and checkout over four payment rails spanning card, crypto, Telegram Wallet Pay and native TON; TON Connect and OKX wallet integration with Telegram initData validated on both ends; merkle-whitelisted drops; a three.js and pixi.js play-to-earn game client; a three-level referral economy; six operator and partner admin consoles, including cross-promotions with Notcoin, W-Coin, Boinkers and City Holder; and the internal design-system package the rest of the team built against. Around it ran a stretch of on-chain contract work — **Horizon DAO**, where I built the entire user-facing dApp for real estate issued as ERC-1155 fractions, from the marketplace and asset pages through the full transaction suite of invest, offer, swap, sell, stake and claim; **Runes Land**, a Bitcoin Runes launchpad I rebuilt from a legacy shell onto Next.js 14; and **Agora AI**, a Telegram Web App for an AI-model marketplace gated by on-chain TON subscriptions.

I started in C++, with two years at **Audaces** on commercial 3D garment-design CAD used by clothing manufacturers worldwide — a C++14, Qt and OpenGL codebase where I worked on the 4D module: the main-window view with symmetry-plane visualisation and an integrated garment library, live garment colouring, and the sculpting and cutting tool interactions.
